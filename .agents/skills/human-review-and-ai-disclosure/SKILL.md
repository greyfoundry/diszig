# human-review-and-ai-disclosure

Read root AGENTS.md first; its invariants apply.

Read the entire diff and applicable tests, evaluate design, correctness, simplicity, licensing, documentation, security and maintainability. Cite concrete findings with paths and impact; distinguish facts from hypotheses. Review generated tests, not just results. Record material AI assistance and actual verification in PR template. Human contributor must understand and review changes; agent cannot approve on human behalf or check their boxes. No unattended PR discussion or bypass of protected checks. Draft or block changes lacking evidence.

Return concrete findings or actual changes, tests, limitations and next action. Do not claim completion without evidence.
